One exploit has real backing

The Apollo Aggregates warehouse respawn (M-01) is the only money exploit with steps published by several outlets, paying about $8,640 to $14,400 per run according to Beebom and GTA BOOM.

It is a Chapter 1 method, and Chapter 1 cash is wiped except $5,000 in Family Savings, per Tech4Gamers.

No car duplication yet

Nobody has shown a working vehicle duplication in GTA VI. The CodeKingGTA research repo lists it as unverified, along with mission replay and fence loop rumors.

Entries M-05 to M-07 stay in the tracker so they can be upgraded when proof appears.

The big money comes later

Large bank vaults pay $65,000 to $205,000 in about five minutes from the end of Chapter 4 and can be repeated after the story, per PowerPyx and Timesaver.GG.

That beats every exploit on this page without any patch risk.

How to read the matrix

Reproducibility

Repeatable
Steps or a trigger are published and more than one source reports it working.
Recurring
Captured by more than one player or clip, but there is no reliable trigger.
Isolated
One clip or one source.
Unverified claim
A claim with no footage or steps.

Patch status

Unpatched
Still reported and no fix has been announced.
Disputed
May be intended behavior or a gameplay setting.
Not a bug
A built-in feature such as a cheat code.
Unverified
Not confirmed to exist, so there is nothing to patch yet.
Not real
A joke, an advertisement or a scam.

Reproducibility is an evidence tier, not a measured success rate. No outlet has published percentage rates for any GTA VI glitch.

GTA VI launched on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, with no PC version or online mode announced, according to Cleveland.com and the SenseOS platform guide. The PC and Online columns stay in the matrix so they can be filled in when that changes.

"Unspecified" means the clip or article does not name the console. Builds 1.002.000 and 1.002.001 shipped before launch without patch notes, per Stealthy Gaming, and Rockstar has not published a known-issues list, per Moneycontrol.
