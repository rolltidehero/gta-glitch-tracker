    How to read the matrix

    Reproducibility

    Repeatable
    Steps or a trigger are published and more than one source reports it working.
    Recurring
    Captured by more than one player or clip, but there is no reliable trigger.
    Isolated
    One clip or one source.
    Unverified claim
    A claim with no footage or steps.

    Patch status

    Unpatched
    Still reported and no fix has been announced.
    Patched
    Patch notes or retests on a newer build say it no longer works. "Partially patched" means only some cases were fixed.
    Disputed
    May be intended behavior or a gameplay setting.
    Not a bug
    A built-in feature such as a cheat code.
    Unverified
    Not confirmed to exist, so there is nothing to patch yet.
    Not real
    A joke, an advertisement or a scam.
